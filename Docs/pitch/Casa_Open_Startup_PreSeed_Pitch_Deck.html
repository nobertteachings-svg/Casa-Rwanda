Casa Kenya Confidential · Pre-seed pitch
The problem

Finding a home in Kenya is slow, risky, and informal.

Tenants (22–40)

Waste days chasing word-of-mouth leads, Facebook posts, and brokers. Pay for transport to visit places already rented or fake. Risk deposits with unverified landlords — especially when they must move in 2–4 weeks.

Landlords (30–55)

Units sit empty. Reach only their immediate network. Face unserious inquiries and lose rental income while waiting for a trustworthy tenant.

  • People rely on friends, street brokers, Facebook groups, WhatsApp statuses, and “To let” signs
  • They love convenience but are sceptical of new apps — especially on slow, expensive internet
Casa Kenya 02 / Problem
The solution

A full housing marketplace inside WhatsApp.

Landlords list. Tenants search. AI assists. Trust is built in. No new app.

1. List

Landlords verify ID, set location, add photos + mandatory property video, and go live in English.

2. Search

Tenants search by neighbourhood, category, or natural language — residential or commercial.

3. Unlock

Pay 5,000 KES (~$3.30) once per listing to get the landlord’s number and map link.

WhatsApp-native AI-assisted ID + video trust English-only NG Paystack / Flutterwave ready

Casa Kenya 06 / Product
Business model

Pay-per-unlock. Free to list. Built for occasional search.

Pricing
  • Tenants: 5,000 KES (~$3.30) per listing unlock
  • Landlords: free to list
  • No agents: landlords and tenants connect directly
  • Launch mode: free unlocks while validating conversion
Unit economics (estimate)
  • CAC: ~$10 once paid social starts (cash CAC $0 today)
  • LTV: ~$25 (≈2 unlocks × 1.5 search cycles)
  • Revenue mix: 100% one-time today; subscriptions later
  • MRR now: $0 — pre-recurring, early traction

Rationale: unlock fee is a small fraction of move-in costs and below what tenants often lose on fake leads and transport.
