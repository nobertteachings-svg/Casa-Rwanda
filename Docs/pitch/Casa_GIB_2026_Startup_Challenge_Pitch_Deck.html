Casa KenyaGIB 2026 · Impact pitch
Problem

Informal housing search locks people out of safe urban opportunity.

Who is affected

Tenants (22–40) and landlords (30–55) in Nairobi, Nairobi and other Kenya cities — plus small businesses seeking commercial space.

What goes wrong

Word of mouth, brokers and Facebook groups waste time and money. Fake or rented-out listings. Unverified landlords. Empty units. No trust layer.

Why it matters at scale

~60% of Kenyans live in cities; urban growth ~3.6%/year. Rental demand is strong because ownership is unaffordable for many (CAHF). Discovery still runs on informal channels while people already live on WhatsApp — but refuse heavy new apps on slow internet.
