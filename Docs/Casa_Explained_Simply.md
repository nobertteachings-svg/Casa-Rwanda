# Casa — Explained in Simple Words

**What is this document?**  
A plain-language guide to Casa for anyone — no technical background needed. It explains what the app does, who it is for, and how every part works.

**Last updated:** June 2026

---

## 1. What is Casa?

**Casa is a way to find and rent homes and commercial spaces in Rwanda using only WhatsApp.**

You do **not** download a new app. You chat with Casa on WhatsApp — the same app most people already use every day.

Casa connects two groups of people:

- **Landlords** — people who have homes, rooms, apartments, or commercial spaces to rent out  
- **Tenants** — people looking for a place to live **or** a commercial space for their business  

Behind the scenes, Casa uses **artificial intelligence (AI)** — a smart computer assistant called **Claude** — to understand messages, help write listings, search for homes, check ID cards, and answer questions.

Casa is built for **Kenya**: prices are in **RWF**, the product is **English-only**, and it understands neighbourhoods like Westlands, Nairobi, Kilimani, and more.

---

## 2. Why does Casa exist?

Finding a home in Rwanda is often hard because:

- People rely on **word of mouth** — you only hear about houses through friends and family  
- **Fake listings** waste time and money  
- **Landlords** struggle to reach tenants outside their area  
- Many people **do not want to install** another app on their phone  

Casa fixes these problems by putting everything on WhatsApp, using **GPS location** to find nearby homes, and using **AI + video + ID checks** to build trust.

## 3. How do you start using Casa?

### Step 1: Open WhatsApp and message Casa

You send a message like **“hello”** or **“menu”** to Casa’s WhatsApp number.

### Step 2: Say who you are

- **1** = I am a **landlord** (I want to rent out my property)  
- **2** = I am a **tenant** (I am looking for a home)  
- **3** = Someone **referred** me (you enter their phone number)

After that, you get a **main menu** with options based on whether you are a landlord or a tenant.

### Step 3: Use the menu

Reply with a **number** (1, 2, 3…) to pick what you want to do.  
You can type **MENU** or **HELP** anytime to go back or get assistance.

### Voice notes

You can send **voice notes** instead of typing. Casa receives them and guides you (full automatic transcription is still being improved).

## 4. For landlords — everything Casa does for you

Landlords are people who own property and want to find tenants.

### 4.1 Before you can list a property

Casa requires two things **before any listing goes live**:

#### A) ID verification (your identity)

- You must send a **clear photo of your Rwandan National ID** or **passport**  
- **Claude (AI) reads the document** and checks:
  - Is it a real ID?  
  - Is it from Rwanda (or a valid passport)?  
  - Is it **expired**?  
  - Is the photo clear enough?  
- If everything looks good → you get a **“Verified landlord”** badge  
- If the ID is bad or expired → you are asked to try again  
- If the photo is unclear → a human on the Casa team may review it  

**How to verify:** Landlord Menu → **3 (More options)** → **7 (Verify my identity)**  
You cannot publish a listing until this is done.

#### B) Video walkthrough (of the property) — required for every listing

- Every listing **must** include a **video** where you walk through the property  
- You show the kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom, compound, and other areas  
- This is **not optional** — no video means no listing  
- Listings with a video get a **“Verified+”** badge (✨) so tenants know the home was actually filmed  

### 4.2 Listing a property — two ways

From the landlord menu, choose **1 (List a new property)**.

#### Way 1: Describe it freely (AI helps)

You type something casual, for example:

> “I have a 2-room apartment in Westlands. Water, light, parking. 80,000 per month, 2 months caution.”

Claude turns that into a clean, professional listing with:

- **Category** — residential (housing) or commercial (for businesses)  
- **Property type** — e.g. single room, studio, 2-bedroom apartment, shop, office  
- Monthly rent  
- Months of rent required upfront  
- District, town, and neighbourhood  
- Facilities (gated/fenced, parking, backup power, borehole/water tank, water supply, electricity — token meter or postpaid REG bill, security/askari, etc.)  
- A short description  

You confirm, then continue with GPS pin, photos, and video.

#### Way 2: Answer questions step by step

The bot asks you one question at a time:

1. **Category** — residential or commercial  
2. **Property type** — depends on category:

   **Residential:**  
   - Single room (shared bathroom / kitchen)  
   - Double room (two rooms, shared facilities)  
   - Bedsitter (room + bathroom + kitchenette)  
   - Studio (open-plan living + sleeping)  
   - 1 bedroom (sitting room + bedroom)  
   - 2 bedroom  
   - 3+ bedroom  
   - Maisonette (multi-level)  
   - Bungalow / standalone house  
   - Servant quarter (SQ / annex)  

   **Commercial:**  
   - Shop / retail space  
   - Office space  
   - Warehouse / go-down  
   - Restaurant / bar / café  
   - Salon / barbershop  
   - Workshop / garage  
   - Showroom / display space  
   - Other commercial space  

3. Monthly rent in RWF  
4. Months upfront required  
5. **Select your district** (e.g. Nairobi, FCT, Rivers)  
6. **Type the city** (e.g. Nairobi, Nairobi)  
7. **Type the neighbourhood** (e.g. Westlands, Westlands)  
8. **Pin your location on WhatsApp (GPS)** — after state, city, and neighbourhood  
9. Gated / fenced compound? Yes/No  
10. Parking? Yes/No  
11. Backup power / generator? Yes/No  
12. Borehole/well? Yes/No  
13. Running water? Yes/No  
14. Electricity meter type — no electricity, prepaid meter, or postpaid meter  
15. Furnished? Yes/No  
16. Security guard? Yes/No  
17. Photos (up to 5)  
18. **Video walkthrough (required)**  
19. Confirm and publish  

### 4.3 Location (state, city, neighbourhood, then GPS)

You first **select your district**, **type the city**, and **type the neighbourhood** — then **pin the exact location** on WhatsApp.  
Casa uses this so tenants can:

- Find homes **near them** or in a specific area  
- See **state, city, and neighbourhood** on the listing  
- Get a **Google Maps link** to navigate to the property  

### 4.4 Photos

You can send up to **5 photos** of the property.  
When done, reply **DONE**.

### 4.5 Video (mandatory)

After photos, you **must** send at least **one video** walking through the property.  
When done, reply **DONE**.

### 4.6 Your listing goes live

You get a **house ID** like **CASA-1001**.  
Tenants can now find your property in search results.

## 5. For tenants — everything Casa does for you

Tenants are people looking for a **residential** home or a **commercial** space (shop, office, warehouse).

### 5.1 Searching for a home

From the tenant menu, choose **1 (Search for a home)**.

First, choose what you are looking for:

- **Residential** (housing)  
- **Commercial** (shop, office, warehouse, restaurant, salon, etc.)  
- **Either** (both types)  

Then you can search in two ways:

#### A) Send your location

Pin your current location on WhatsApp. Casa finds matching properties **near you** (within about 5 km by default).

#### B) Describe what you want in your own words

Examples:

> “I need a quiet 2-bedroom apartment near a school in Westlands, max 60k, with parking and water.”  
> “Commercial shop space in Westlands, Nairobi, with parking, max 150k.”

Claude understands:

- Residential or commercial  
- Specific property type (studio, single room, shop, etc.)  
- Area / state / city / neighbourhood  
- Maximum rent  
- Facilities you need (gated, parking, backup power, borehole/tank, water, token/postpaid meter, askari, etc.)  

You can search in **English**.

### 5.2 Search results

Casa shows matching properties with:

- House ID (e.g. CASA-1001)  
- Category (residential or commercial) and property type  
- District, town, and neighbourhood  
- Monthly rent  
- Months required upfront  
- Distance from you  
- Key facilities (gated, parking, backup power, borehole/tank, water, electricity meter type, askari, etc.)  
- **Verified landlord** badge if the owner passed ID check  
- **Verified+** badge if the listing has a required video walkthrough  

Reply with a **number** to see full details.

### 5.3 Total cost calculator

Before you pay, Casa shows a clear breakdown:

- Monthly rent  
- Upfront months (e.g. 2 months caution = 2 × rent)  
- Casa unlock fee (**5,000 RWF**)  
- **Grand total: “You need X RWF to move in”**  

This helps you avoid surprises.

### 5.4 Unlocking the landlord’s contact (the main payment)

To get the landlord’s phone number:

1. You pay **5,000 RWF** via **Paystack** or **Flutterwave** (card, bank transfer, or USSD)  
2. Use the **house ID** (e.g. CASA-1001) as the payment reference  
3. Reply **PAID**  

**Important:** Full automatic payment checking with Paystack/Flutterwave is still being connected. Right now, the system may unlock after you confirm payment — production will verify payments automatically.

After unlock you receive:

- Landlord’s **WhatsApp phone number**  
- **Google Maps link** to navigate to the property  

You only pay **once per house** — if you already unlocked it, you can see the contact again for free.

### 5.5 After you unlock — concierge help

Casa automatically sends you:

1. **Visit checklist** — what to check when you go see the house (water pressure, prepaid/postpaid meter, mould, noise, etc.)  
2. **Negotiation tips** — practical advice for Rwanda rental culture  
3. **Documents list** — ID, lease, receipts, deposit proof, etc.  
4. Option to reply **LEASE** for an AI-generated rental agreement template  

### 5.6 Tenant menu — all options

| Option | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **1. Search for a home** | Find properties by location or description |
| **2. My unlocked contacts** | List of landlords you already paid to contact |
| **3. More options** | Extra tools (see below) |
| **4. Help** | Tips and support |

### 5.7 Tenant “More options” menu (option 3)

| # | Feature | What it does in simple words |
|---|---------|------------------------------|
| 1 | **Saved search alert** | Describe your dream home once; Casa messages you when a new match is listed |
| 2 | **Compare shortlist** | Save 2–3 houses (reply **SAVE** on a listing), then get an AI comparison |
| 3 | **Diaspora mode** | If you live abroad, search and pay for a home for family in Rwanda — **both numbers** get the contact and map link |
| 4 | **Get verified badge** | Upload ID or link Paystack — shows landlords you are a serious, verified tenant |
| 5 | **Refer a friend** | Invite someone; earn a **free unlock credit** when they complete a transaction |
| 6 | **Rent heat map** | See average rents by neighbourhood (based on active listings on Casa) |

### 5.8 Other things tenants can do on a listing

When viewing a property’s details:

- **SAVE** — add to your shortlist for comparison  
- **FLAG** — report if the listing seems fake or misleading (goes to Casa moderation)  

### 5.9 Referral credits

If you referred someone, or someone referred you, you can earn a **free unlock** (skip the 5,000 RWF fee once). Credits expire after 90 days.

## 6. Trust and safety — how Casa fights scams

| Feature | Who it helps | How it works |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Landlord ID check** | Tenants | Landlord must show National ID/passport; AI checks validity and expiry |
| **Required property video** | Tenants | Every listing must include a walkthrough video (Verified+) |
| **Verified landlord badge** | Tenants | Shown on listings when owner passed ID check |
| **Verified tenant badge** | Landlords | Tenants can verify identity; shown when they browse |
| **Community flagging** | Everyone | Tenants who visited can report misleading listings |
| **Moderation queue** | Casa team | Flagged listings reviewed in admin dashboard |
| **Listing status** | Casa team | Listings can be active, inactive, flagged, or under review |

Claude can also (when fully enabled) scan listings for red flags like rent too low for the area, fake photos, or bad GPS locations.

## 7. The AI (Claude) — what it does for you

You never need to talk to “Claude” directly. It works quietly in the background:

| Task | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Write listings** | Turns a casual WhatsApp message into a proper property ad |
| **Understand searches** | “Cheap 2-bedroom near Kilimani with water” → structured search |
| **Compare homes** | Side-by-side summary of 2–3 shortlisted properties |
| **Check landlord IDs** | Reads ID photo, checks expiry, approves or rejects |
| **Suggest rent prices** | Tells landlords if their price may be too high for the area |
| **Generate leases** | Draft rental agreement in English |
| **Answer after unlock** | Visit tips, negotiation advice, document lists |
| **Market reports** | “Average rent in Westlands moved up 5%” |

Claude needs an API key configured on the server. Without it, some AI features fall back to simpler behaviour or manual review.

## 10. Casa for people without smartphones (USSD)

For **basic phones** without WhatsApp, Casa offers a simple **USSD** text menu (like `*123#` style services — needs partnership with a mobile network).

Via USSD you can:

- **Search** for homes in a broad area (limited compared to WhatsApp)  
- **Cannot fully list** a property — you are directed to WhatsApp for ID + video requirements  

This helps reach rural users, but the full experience is on WhatsApp.

## 12. What a typical day looks like

### Landlord example

1. Marie messages Casa on WhatsApp  
2. Registers as landlord  
3. Sends photo of her National ID → AI approves → **Verified landlord**  
4. Lists her apartment: chooses **residential** → **2-room apartment** → rent and upfront months  
5. Selects **FCT** state, types **Nairobi** and **Westlands**, then pins GPS  
6. Answers facilities (fenced, parking, **prepaid meter**, etc.)  
7. Sends 3 photos and 1 walkthrough video  
8. Listing **CASA-1042** goes live with **Verified+**  
9. Next week she gets: “CASA-1042 got 8 views and 1 unlock”  
10. A tenant pays 5,000 RWF; Marie gets the tenant’s interest (notification when payments fully wired)  

### Tenant example

1. Jean messages Casa in English, registers as tenant  
2. Chooses **Search** → **Residential**  
3. Sends location in Nairobi  
4. Types: “2-bedroom max 75k with water, parking, and prepaid meter”  
5. Sees 4 matches, picks one, sees total move-in cost  
6. Pays 5,000 RWF, replies PAID  
7. Gets landlord number + Google Maps link  
8. Receives visit checklist and negotiation tips automatically  
9. Visits the house; if it was fake, he replies **FLAG**  

### Diaspora example

1. Paul lives in France, registers as tenant  
2. More options → Diaspora mode → enters his mother’s Rwanda number  
3. Searches for a house in Nairobi, pays unlock  
4. **Paul and his mother** both receive the landlord contact and map link

## 13. House IDs and data Casa stores

Each property gets a unique ID: **CASA-1000**, **CASA-1001**, etc.

Casa stores (securely on servers):

- Phone numbers and language preference  
- Listing details: category (residential/commercial), property type, state, city, neighbourhood, rent, facilities (including **electricity meter type**: none, prepaid, or postpaid), photos, videos  
- Who unlocked which contact and when  
- ID verification records (for landlords)  
- Search alerts, shortlists, referrals, listing views, and flags
